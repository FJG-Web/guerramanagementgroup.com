Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Guerra Management Group Corp. · RFC 9116

We take the security of Guerra Management Group Corp. seriously and welcome responsible disclosure from the community. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in our website or services, please let us know.

How to report

  • Email: security@guerramanagementgroup.com
  • Machine-readable policy: /.well-known/security.txt

Please include steps to reproduce, affected URL(s), and any proof-of-concept. We aim to acknowledge reports within one business day and will keep you updated on remediation progress.

Please do not

  • Access, modify, or delete data that is not your own.
  • Run automated scanning or load testing that degrades service for others.
  • Publicly disclose a vulnerability before we have had a chance to remediate it.

Scope

  • https://www.guerramanagementgroup.com
  • The contact API at guerra-contact-api.vercel.app

Out of scope

  • Findings requiring physical access or social engineering of staff.
  • Volumetric / denial-of-service testing.
  • Reports affecting only outdated or unsupported browsers.

Acting in good faith under this policy, we will not pursue or support legal action against you for your research. Thank you for helping keep the systems people rely on secure.
